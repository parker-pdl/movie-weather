Privacy Policy for Parker Weather

Effective Date: August 8, 2026

Thank you for using Parker Weather (also distributed as "Movie Weather" / "Parker Data Link Weather"), a movie-themed weather app. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app accesses, how it's used, and the choices you have.

1. Location Information

To show accurate local weather, the app requests access to your device's location (or estimates your general location from your IP address if permission is denied). This is used only to:

  • Determine your current location for weather forecasts.
  • Provide relevant current conditions, hourly, and daily forecasts.

We do not sell, rent, or otherwise share your precise location with advertisers.

2. Advertising

The free version of the app displays ads served through Google AdSense. Google and its advertising partners may collect and use technical data (such as device identifiers, general location, and app usage) to serve and measure ads, in accordance with Google's own privacy practices. You can remove ads at any time with the one-time in-app "Remove Ads" purchase, after which no ad-related data is collected by the app.

3. Weather Data

Forecast data is retrieved from third-party weather data providers based on your location. We do not control the privacy practices of those providers.

4. What We Don't Do

The app does not require account creation, does not collect your name, email, or other personal identifiers, and does not include any user-generated content or user-to-user communication features.

5. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to Paul@parkerdatalink.com.
